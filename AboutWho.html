Who We Are

We are spirit filled congegration that has a core believes in the Statements of faith...

    Statements of Faith

  • The One True God – We believe in one God eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit
  • The Deity of the Lord Jesus Christ – We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man.
  • The Fall of Man – We believe humanity fell into sin through disobedience to God.
  • The Salvation of Man – We believe salvation is received through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • The Ordinances of the Church – We practice water baptism and Holy Communion.
  • The Baptism in the Holy Spirit – We believe in the empowering baptism of the Holy Spirit.
  • The Initial Physical Evidence of Spirit Baptism – We believe speaking in tongues is the initial evidence.
  • Sanctification – We believe believers are called to holy living.
  • The Church and Its Mission – We believe the Church exists to evangelize and disciple nations.
  • The Ministry – We believe God calls and equips ministers for service
  • Divine Healing – We believe God heals according to His will and promises.
  • The Millennial Reign of Christ – We believe Christ will reign on earth for a thousand years.
  • The Final Judgment – We believe every person will stand before God in judgment.
  • The New Heavens and the New Earth – We believe God will establish an eternal kingdom of righteousness.

Meet Our Pastor

Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard is the Founder and General Overseer of Temple Victory Ministry International, a devoted servant of God whose life has been marked by faith, dedication, compassion, and an unwavering commitment to the work of the Gospel. Born as the fourth child in a family of six children, Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard was raised with strong values that would later shape her character, leadership, and passion for serving God and humanity. From an early age, she demonstrated a deep love for God and a desire to live according to His purpose. Her journey with Christ began at the tender age of eleven, when she surrendered her life completely to the Lord. Since that time, she has remained steadfast in her Christian faith, growing spiritually and faithfully serving God through various stages of her life. Her testimony is one of God's grace, faithfulness, preservation, and divine guidance through the years. Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard is an ordained Reverend under the Assemblies of God and has devoted her life to preaching the Gospel, teaching the Word of God, nurturing believers, and leading souls to Christ. Through her ministry, many lives have experienced spiritual transformation, restoration, healing, and renewed hope in God. In addition to her ministerial calling, Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard is a woman of remarkable educational and professional accomplishment. She is a Registered Nurse, a Licensed Teacher, and a respected Christian leader who has consistently used her knowledge, skills, and experience to serve both God and humanity. Her commitment to excellence in both ministry and professional service reflects her belief that Christians should positively impact every area of society. As a spiritual leader, she is known for her passion for prayer, holiness, sound biblical teaching, and genuine concern for the spiritual growth of God's people. Her messages emphasize repentance, salvation, righteous living, faith, obedience to God, and preparation for eternity. She continually encourages believers to develop a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ and to remain faithful regardless of life's challenges. Through divine inspiration and a burden for souls, Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard founded Temple Victory Ministry International with a vision of raising disciples, restoring lives, building strong Christian families, and preparing people for the Kingdom of God. Under her leadership, the ministry has become a place where individuals encounter God's presence, receive biblical instruction, and experience spiritual growth. Beyond her role as a pastor and spiritual mentor, Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard is a devoted wife and loving mother. She is happily married to Mr. Longinus Richard, whose support, partnership, and commitment have been instrumental throughout her journey in ministry. Together, they have built a God-centered family and continue to serve as an example of Christian faith, love, and dedication. God has blessed their union with three wonderful children: Great Richard, Mighty Richard, and Perfect Richard. Her family remains a source of joy, strength, and encouragement as she continues to fulfill her calling in ministry. Those who know Reverend Mrs. Phoebe Richard often describe her as compassionate, prayerful, humble, disciplined, caring, and deeply committed to the things of God. Her life serves as an inspiration to believers, especially women who desire to walk faithfully with God while fulfilling their God-given purpose. Today, she continues to lead Temple Victory Ministry International with wisdom, faith, and a heart for people. Her desire is to see lives transformed by the power of God, families restored, communities impacted by the Gospel, and believers prepared for the glorious return of our Lord Jesus Christ. "A faithful servant, a compassionate leader, a devoted wife and mother, and a woman whose life continues to shine as a testimony of God's grace and faithfulness.”
