TEMPLE OF VICTORY MINISTRIES INT'L

About Our Church

Welcome to Temple Victory Ministry International, a Pentecostal church committed to raising believers who live holy lives, walk in the power of God, and remain steadfast on the journey to Heaven. Located in Umuahia, Abia State, Nigeria, Temple Victory Ministry International is more than a place of worship—it is a spiritual family, a house of prayer, a center of transformation, and a sanctuary where lives are touched by the presence and power of God. Although Temple Victory Ministry International was officially established in 2013, the vision and ministry work began years earlier in 2006 through the divine calling and dedication of our Founder and General Overseer, a woman of faith whom God has used as an instrument of revival, restoration, and spiritual growth. Through her obedience to God's calling, countless lives have been impacted, souls have been won for Christ, and believers have been strengthened in their walk with God. For over a decade, our ministry has remained committed to the unchanging truth of God's Word. We believe that the Gospel of Jesus Christ is the power of God unto salvation and that every believer is called to live a life of holiness, righteousness, and complete devotion to God. Our mission is not merely to gather people in a church building but to prepare hearts for eternity and equip believers to become true disciples of Christ. At Temple Victory Ministry International, we firmly uphold biblical principles and standards of Christian living. We teach and encourage modesty, holiness, godly character, and separation from worldly influences. Our members are taught to reflect Christ not only in worship but also in their daily conduct, appearance, speech, and lifestyle. We believe that holiness is not a burden but a beautiful expression of our love and obedience to God. Our church is built upon the foundation of prayer, faith, sound doctrine, evangelism, and the leading of the Holy Spirit. Through powerful worship services, fervent prayers, Bible teachings, revival meetings, and outreach programs, we strive to bring people into a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ and help them discover God's purpose for their lives. We are passionate about seeing lives transformed by the power of God. We believe that no situation is beyond God's ability to change. Through faith in Jesus Christ, the broken can be restored, the lost can be found, the weak can become strong, and every believer can experience victory through God's grace. Our vision is to raise a generation of believers who will stand firm in truth, shine as lights in a dark world, and remain faithful until the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. We desire to see families restored, communities transformed, and souls prepared for the Kingdom of God. As a ministry, we continue to uphold the message of repentance, salvation, holiness, sanctification, and the baptism of the Holy Spirit. We believe that Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever, and we remain committed to proclaiming His Gospel with boldness and compassion. Whether you are seeking spiritual growth, a deeper understanding of God's Word, a place to worship, or a church family that genuinely cares for your spiritual well-being, Temple Victory Ministry International welcomes you with open arms. Join us as we worship God, grow in faith, serve with love, and pursue our ultimate goal—to make Heaven and help others make Heaven.

Our Mission

To spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ, disciple believers, and impact our community through love, service, and faith.

Our Vision

Raising disciples, transforming lives, and advancing the Kingdom of God.
