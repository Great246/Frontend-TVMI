Our Ministries

Our congegration is a place that is filled with love and everyone is believed to have a place to belong

WOMEN'S FELLOWSHIP

The Women Fellowship of Temple of Victory Ministries is a community of women growing together in faith, prayer, love, and purpose. We encourage and equip women to become godly influences in their families, church, workplaces, and communities..

MEN'S FELLOWSHIP

The Men Fellowship of Temple of Victory Ministries is committed to raising godly men of faith, integrity, responsibility, and leadership. Through fellowship, prayer, and God's Word, we encourage men to grow spiritually and become positive influences in their families and society.

YOUTH'S FELLOWSHIP

The Youth Fellowship of Temple of Victory Ministries is a vibrant community where young people discover their identity, develop their gifts, and grow in their relationship with Christ. We inspire and equip the next generation to live with faith, purpose, and confidence.

CHILDREN'S FELLOWSHIP

The Children Fellowship of Temple of Victory Ministries provides a joyful and nurturing environment where children learn about God's love and grow in faith. Through Bible teaching, worship, prayer, and fun activities, we help children build a strong foundation in Christ.
